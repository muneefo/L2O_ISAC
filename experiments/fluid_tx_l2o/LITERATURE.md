# Closest-source screen (2026-10-26)

This is a focused screen, not a claim of comprehensive novelty. Descriptions below distinguish inspected content from open verification tasks.

- **Wang, Vorobyov, Ollila (TWC2026), DOI10.1109/TWC.2026.3702925.** The retained full paper extraction is available locally and its model, Algorithm1/2, Eq22 and appendices were checked. The inherited method is layerwise step/momentum unrolling of MinNet–MaxNet, not an architecture invented here. Our contribution under investigation is its coupled spatial-channel/position extension, with exact spacing projection and independent evaluation. Institutional metadata/link: https://research.aalto.fi/en/publications/l2o-robust-hybrid-beamforming-for-isac/ . Official PDF access failed403; no missing access is concealed.

- **Yang et al., “Towards Intelligent Antenna Positioning: Leveraging DRL for FAS-Aided ISAC Systems,” arXiv2501.01281.** Inspected the author full-text HTML system model and BCD/DDPG formulation: https://arxiv.org/html/2501.01281v1 . It includes continuous two-dimensional BS/user-side position variables, communication-rate and sensing-gain design, with covariance optimization and DDPG position actions. Continuous position learning for ISAC therefore already exists. Its physical variables/objective/optimizer differ from this fixed-user, fixed-radar-Rx, one-dimensional hybrid norm-bounded MinNet–MaxNet extension. A numerical DDPG baseline cannot be treated as directly comparable without re-formulating it for our constraints; we do not fabricate such results.

- **J. Zhang et al., “Generative reinforcement learning for fluid antenna systems based sensing and communication,” npj Wireless Technology2,30 (2026), DOI10.1038/s44459-026-00035-0.** Inspected the publisher article's framework/system description: https://www.nature.com/articles/s44459-026-00035-0 . It combines a primal-dual beamforming network with generative PPO for port selection on a discrete planar FAS. It is not evidence that all joint FAS/beamforming learning is new; port-selection results are not a matched continuous-position benchmark for our experiment.

- **Hao et al., “Fluid-Antenna Enhanced ISAC: Joint Antenna Positioning and Dual-Functional Beamforming Design under Perfect and Imperfect CSI,” arXiv2407.18988; published DOI10.1109/TVT.2025.3574072.** The author abstract was inspected in earlier draft work, not a new full-text reproduction here. It describes robust joint geometry/beamforming with sensing-SNR and user-SINR constraints. Robust fluid ISAC itself is prior art. Before submission, a full objective/hardware/uncertainty comparison and an adapted strong classical AO baseline remain desirable.

- **Zhou et al., WCL2024, DOI10.1109/LWC.2024.3476148.** Author-deposited source linked at https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10199665/ ; already considered in the initial draft. Fluid BS/user ISAC and AO are prior art, not the new claim.

Remaining source work: full-text comparison to DOI10.1109/TCOMM.2026.3663183 (robust movable-antenna ISAC) and wider continuous-position unfolding literature. No “first,” demonstrated novelty, or acceptance claim is made. The current numerical comparisons isolate learning/geometry/momentum within the inherited architecture; they are not a completed cross-literature performance comparison.
